Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information this website collects, what we do with it, and what rights you have over it. We have tried to write it in plain English rather than legal language.

Who we are

This website is run by Knightcott Self Storage. We are the "data controller" for the information described below, which means we are responsible for looking after it.

  • Knightcott Self Storage, Unit 7 Knightcott Industrial Estate, Banwell, North Somerset, BS29 6JN
  • Telephone: 01934 822420
  • Email: info@knightcottselfstorage.uk

What we collect, and when

The only personal information this website collects is what you type into the enquiry form, and only when you choose to send it. That is:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give it — this one is optional
  • What you are looking to store

That is everything. If you do not use the form, the website collects nothing about you at all. We do not keep web access logs.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind — nothing that follows you around, and nothing that builds a profile of you. That is also why you have not been asked to accept a cookie banner: there is nothing to accept.

Why we use your information, and our lawful basis

We use what you send us for one purpose only: to read your enquiry and reply to it. We may contact you by email or phone to answer your question or discuss a unit.

Under UK data protection law we must have a "lawful basis" for using your information. Ours is legitimate interests — specifically, our interest in responding to an enquiry that you have chosen to send us, which is also the reason you sent it. Where an enquiry leads to you renting a unit, we also use your details to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We do not have a mailing list and we will not add you to one. We do not send marketing, and we do not sell or rent your details to anyone. No decisions are made about you automatically.

Who your information is shared with

When you send the form, your message is emailed to info@knightcottselfstorage.uk. It is not saved into any database, customer system or spreadsheet by the website.

That email is sent using Amazon Web Services (AWS), on their servers in London. AWS also hosts this website. AWS acts as our "processor", which means they handle the information strictly on our instructions and for no purpose of their own.

Nobody else receives your information. We do not share it with any other company.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as they are still useful — for example while we are talking to you about a unit, or in case you get back in touch — and then we delete them. We do not keep them indefinitely, and we do not keep them for any fixed period beyond that.

If you would like us to delete your enquiry sooner, just ask and we will.

Links to other websites

This website contains one link to another site: a plain text link to our reviews on Google. If you click it you are taken to Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to what happens there. Nothing from Google is built into or embedded in our pages, so Google receives nothing about you unless you choose to click that link.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct it if it is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete it
  • Restrict what we do with it
  • Object to us using it

To ask for any of these, email info@knightcottselfstorage.uk, call 01934 822420, or write to us at the address above. It is free, and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first and we will do our best to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how any of this works, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top.
